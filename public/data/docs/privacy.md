> This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use Rex, the Dashboard and the website. Rex is an independent third-party Discord bot and is not an official Discord product.

## 1. Who is responsible
The Rex team (the operators of Rex) is the controller of personal data handled through the Service. Contact us through the [Support Server](support:) or the [Support page](/support) for any privacy question or request.

## 2. Information we handle
| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Discord account details | User ID, username, avatar, and your email address if you allow it when signing in | Identify you, show your profile, send emails you have not opted out of |
| Your servers | List of servers you can manage and your permissions in them (from Discord sign-in) | Show only the servers you can manage |
| Server settings and content | Settings you save, welcome and leave messages, ticket setup, custom commands, levels and XP data | Provide the features you configure |
| Moderation and logs | Server IDs, moderator and member IDs, actions and log entries the server enables | Run moderation and logging features |
| Tier and codes | Server tier, codes you redeem, trials | Apply entitlements |
| Session data | A sign-in session cookie and security tokens | Keep you signed in and protect against forged requests |
| Support | Support chat messages, answers you give to Rex, and any ticket you open in the Support Server | Help you and keep a record |
| Emails | Your email preferences and a log of emails sent | Send updates you have chosen to receive |
| Technical data | Error logs, request and performance information, rate-limit counters | Keep the service secure, reliable and free of abuse |

We do not ask for passwords, payment card numbers or bot tokens. Do not send them to us.

## 3. How we use information
We use personal data to:
- provide and operate the features you use;
- apply permissions and subscription entitlements;
- secure the Service and prevent spam and abuse;
- diagnose errors and improve reliability;
- respond to support requests;
- send emails you have not opted out of, and essential service notices.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Legal bases
Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of our agreement with you (providing the Service), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improvement), your consent (for example optional emails and where you share information voluntarily), and legal obligations.

## 5. Where data is stored and who processes it
The Service runs on third-party providers who process data for us, including **Cloudflare** (website, Worker and storage), our bot hosting provider, **Discord** (sign-in and the platform itself) and, where emails are enabled, **Google** (Gmail, used to send emails). They process data only as needed to provide their services, under their own terms and privacy policies. Discord's policy is at [discord.com/privacy](https://discord.com/privacy). Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

## 6. Retention
We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above:
- **Website data tied to your account** stays until you delete it from your Profile or ask us to.
- **Support chats:** closed chats are removed automatically **30 days** after closing unless we need to keep them for safety, abuse prevention or legal reasons. Deleting a chat from your own list hides it for you only; it is removed for everyone at the 30 day point.
- **Sessions** expire automatically.
- **Server data** is removed when Rex is removed from a server or the server's owner asks us to delete it.
- Security, abuse-prevention and legal records may be kept longer where needed.

## 7. Sharing
We share personal data only with providers who help us run the Service (section 5), with Discord as part of normal bot operation, where you or a server you administer asks us to, to protect safety and security, or where the law requires it. In a server, features such as moderation logs, welcome messages and levels are visible to that server's members and staff according to that server's own settings. We are not responsible for how server owners use data in their servers.

## 8. Security
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, including encrypted connections, access limits for owner tools, and limited-lifetime sessions. No online service is perfectly secure. Report issues under the [Security Policy](/docs/security).

## 9. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can:
- delete what the website stores about you from your **Profile**;
- change email choices on your Profile or through the preferences link in any email;
- contact us through the [Support Server](support:) for any other request.

We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. Children
You must meet Discord's minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the minimum age. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Cookies
We use a sign-in session cookie and small browser storage items for essentials such as your chosen theme and unsent drafts. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. We will give notice of material changes through official channels.

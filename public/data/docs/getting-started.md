## 1. What you need
- A Discord account and the **Manage Server** permission in the server where you want Rex.
- A modern web browser for the [Dashboard](/dashboard).

## 2. Add Rex to your server
1. Press **Add Rex** on the [home page](/) or in the [Dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Choose your server and approve the permissions Discord shows.
3. Rex joins and is ready to use straight away.

> **Permissions:** Rex works best with *Administrator*. At minimum it needs View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Manage Roles, Manage Channels and Moderate Members. Do not grant more than you are comfortable with, and never share your bot token with anyone.

## 3. Check Rex's role
Rex's role must sit **above** any role it needs to manage (for example verification or level reward roles). Drag it up in *Server Settings, Roles*. Run `/botperms` in Discord for a quick permissions check.

## 4. Open the Dashboard
1. Go to the [Dashboard](/dashboard) and press **Log in** (Discord sign-in).
2. Approve the access Discord asks for (see the [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy) for what this is used for).
3. Pick your server. Servers only appear if you have *Manage Server* there and Rex is inside it.
4. Use the side menu to configure features. Changes reach the bot within a couple of seconds.

## 5. Explore commands
Type `/` in any channel where Rex can see and send messages, then pick a Rex command. The full list, with who can run each command, is on the [Commands page](/commands). See [Commands and Settings](/docs/commands) for more.

## 6. Get help
- Check [Troubleshooting and Support](/docs/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/docs/faq).
- Join the [Rex Support Server](support:). Joining for the first time also gives your account a **7 day Premium trial** (check your DMs for the activation button).

> **Security reminder:** Rex staff will never ask for your password, authentication codes or bot token.

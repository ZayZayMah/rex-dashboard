## 1. Scope
This policy covers the Rex Discord bot, the Rex website and Dashboard, and related services run by the Rex team. It explains how to report vulnerabilities and what we expect from users.

## 2. Reporting a vulnerability
Report suspected vulnerabilities **privately** through the [Rex Support Server](support:) by opening a ticket, or through the [Support page](/support). Please include:
1. a clear description of the issue;
2. the affected feature or service;
3. the potential impact;
4. steps to reproduce it and any evidence;
5. any error messages (but no secrets).

Do not post sensitive technical details in public channels.

## 3. Responsible disclosure rules
When researching or reporting you must **not**:
- access another person's account or private data;
- extract, alter or delete data without authorisation;
- disrupt Rex or its hosting infrastructure, or run denial-of-service or automated scanning at volume;
- use a vulnerability to gain privileges or paid features;
- publish exploitable details before we have had a reasonable chance to fix them.

Test only systems and accounts you own or have explicit permission to test. This policy does not authorise testing Discord, Cloudflare or any other third party. You must comply with the law.

If you follow these rules and act in good faith, we will treat your research as authorised under this policy and will not pursue action against you for it. Breaking these rules may result in access restrictions and, where appropriate, being reported to the authorities.

## 4. What happens next
We review reports based on severity and impact and may investigate, fix the issue, restrict affected functionality or publish an update. We aim to acknowledge reports promptly, but we do not guarantee response or fix times, or rewards.

## 5. Protecting your account
Never share:
- Discord passwords or authentication codes;
- bot tokens or API keys;
- dashboard session credentials.

If a token or credential is exposed, revoke or reset it immediately through Discord's developer portal or account settings. Only open the Dashboard from official Rex links, and check the permissions requested whenever you authorise an application.

## 6. Suspicious activity
Report unexpected access to restricted features, unusual sign-in activity, unauthorised setting changes, possible data exposure or odd bot behaviour. Do not include passwords or tokens in your report.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

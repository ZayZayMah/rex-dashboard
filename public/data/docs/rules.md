## 1. Introduction and scope
These rules apply to everyone who uses Rex, the Rex Dashboard, the Rex website or official Rex community spaces, including the [Support Server](support:). By using them you agree to follow these rules, the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms), and Discord's [Terms](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

## 2. Follow Discord's rules
Activity that breaks Discord's policies is not allowed just because it involves a third-party bot. Discord may act independently under its own policies.

## 3. Respect other people
You must not:
- harass, bully or target people for abuse;
- make threats or intimidate others;
- engage in hate-based abuse or discriminatory harassment;
- deliberately disrupt conversations or events;
- share someone's private information without permission.

Disagreement is fine. Abuse is not.

## 4. No spam or abuse of features
You must not:
- spam commands, messages or support requests;
- flood channels with repeated content;
- use automation to harass users;
- evade restrictions to continue disruptive behaviour;
- abuse bot features in a way that disrupts others or the service.

Respect Discord's rate limits.

## 5. No exploits or unauthorised access
You must not attempt to compromise Rex, its Dashboard, hosting or data. This includes exploiting vulnerabilities, accessing other people's accounts or data, interfering with operations, using stolen credentials or tokens, or deliberately abusing bugs for unfair benefit. Report vulnerabilities privately under the [Security Policy](/docs/security).

## 6. Keep accounts secure and don't impersonate
- Never share passwords, authentication codes or bot tokens.
- Do not pretend to be Rex staff or to represent the Rex team.
- Only trust official links published by Rex.

## 7. Tiers, codes and billing
You must not exploit subscription systems, abuse redemption codes or obtain paid features by unauthorised means. See [Plans and Billing](/docs/plans).

## 8. Responsible use
You are responsible for how you use Rex. Do not use it for scams, malicious automation, unauthorised data collection, harassment or any unlawful purpose. Server owners are responsible for configuring Rex and permissions sensibly in their own servers and for how their staff use it.

## 9. Support and reports
Contact the team through the [Support page](/support) or [Support Server](support:). Give a clear description and relevant evidence, and do not post passwords, tokens, private information or sensitive security details publicly. Knowingly false or misleading reports break these rules.

## 10. Enforcement
Depending on severity, history and risk, we may:
1. warn you;
2. remove content from Rex community spaces;
3. temporarily restrict your access to Rex, the Dashboard, support chat or community spaces;
4. permanently remove you from official community spaces or restrict your access to Rex;
5. report serious conduct to Discord or the relevant authorities.

We may act without prior warning where the conduct is serious, repeated or a security risk. You can ask for a review through the Support Server, and we will consider it fairly, but we are not obliged to reverse a decision.

## 11. Changes
We may update these rules. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Continuing to use Rex after a change means you accept the updated rules.

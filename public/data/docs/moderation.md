## 1. What moderation is
Moderation means enforcing your server's rules and keeping the community safe. Rex gives authorised staff tools for this. The commands available depend on your server's tier and permissions. See the [Commands page](/commands) for the current list.

## 2. Before you act
1. Read your server's rules.
2. Gather context and avoid acting on rumours.
3. Confirm you have the right Discord permission or staff role.
4. Follow your server's staff procedure.
5. Pick an action proportionate to the situation.

## 3. Good practice
- **Be fair:** apply rules consistently to everyone.
- **Be professional:** keep staff discussions respectful.
- **Protect privacy:** never publicly share private messages or personal information.
- **Keep records:** note significant decisions and the reasons, and use Rex's logs (*Logs* in the Dashboard).
- **Be proportionate:** warnings and timeouts before bans, unless the situation is serious.

## 4. Permissions
Server administrators should:
- give Rex only the permissions it needs, with its role above the roles it manages;
- set staff roles under *Roles and Permissions* in the Dashboard;
- review permissions if moderation stops working;
- never share bot tokens or other secrets.

## 5. Handling rule breaks
1. Review the messages or behaviour.
2. Identify which rule applies.
3. Follow your warning and escalation process.
4. Use the matching action (warn, timeout, kick or ban) if needed.
5. Record the decision.

For threats, harassment, scams or anything illegal, follow Discord's policies and report it to Discord as well. You can find them at [discord.com/guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

## 6. Appeals
When Rex DMs someone about a ban it can include an **Appeal Ban** button. Each server decides how many appeals a person can send and who answers them. Rex's team cannot lift a ban in a server it does not run.

## 7. Misuse of moderation tools
You must not use moderation tools to target people over personal disagreements, punish without a valid reason, harass or intimidate, bypass Discord's rules or safety systems, or access information without authorisation. Only trusted, authorised staff should use them. Server owners are responsible for their staff's use of Rex in their server.

## 8. If a moderation command fails
Check that:
- Rex has the required Discord permissions;
- Rex's role is above the target's highest role (Discord role hierarchy);
- the command exists in the current version (see the [Commands page](/commands));
- you entered the options correctly;
- Discord or Rex is not having a temporary problem (see [Live stats](/live)).

If it still fails, see [Troubleshooting and Support](/docs/troubleshooting).

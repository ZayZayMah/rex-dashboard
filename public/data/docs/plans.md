## 1. The four tiers
| Tier | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Core Rex features and standard commands. |
| **Premium** | Adds AutoMod, multiple ticket types, anti-ping roles and the custom command builder (5 commands and 5 events). |
| **Priority** | Everything in Premium, unlimited custom commands and events, faster support and priority-only features. |
| **Ultra** | Everything in Priority, plus your own bot name, picture and banner. |

The [Premium page](/premium) always shows the current feature list. Tier features can change as Rex is updated.

## 2. How tiers apply
- Tiers are attached to a **server**. Only the server's own tier counts for its features.
- A tier does not give anyone Discord administrator permissions or access to unrelated services.
- If a feature is unavailable, check the server's tier and the command's requirements first.

## 3. Redemption codes
1. Open the [Premium page](/premium) or your server's Premium page.
2. Paste the code and press **Redeem**.

Codes are one use per server unless the code says otherwise. By using a code you agree that:
- it is subject to its stated tier, duration, expiry date and usage limit;
- you will keep unused codes private and will not sell, share or attempt to bypass limits on them;
- we may cancel a code or its benefits if it was obtained or used in breach of these terms.

If a valid code does not work, contact the [Support Server](support:).

## 4. Free trial and promotions
Joining the [Support Server](support:) for the first time gives your account a **7 day Premium trial**. You activate it on one server using the button Rex sends you.

Promotions may have eligibility rules, limits and an end date shown when they are offered. A promotion is not permanent or renewable unless it explicitly says so.

## 5. Transfers
Where the system allows it, a tier can be moved between eligible servers or accounts. Transfers must follow the rules shown by Rex or given by the Rex team. You must not bypass ownership checks or use another person's tier without their permission.

## 6. Payments, renewals and refunds
Any paid tier will state its price, billing period, renewal terms and cancellation process **before you pay**. By completing a purchase you agree to the terms shown at that time. Unless those purchase terms say otherwise:
- Paid access lasts for the period you paid for and ends when it expires.
- You are responsible for any taxes or fees charged by your payment provider.
- Refunds are handled case by case by the Rex team through the Support Server.

Nothing in this page limits consumer rights that apply to you by law and cannot be excluded, including any statutory right to cancel or to a remedy for faulty digital content.

## 7. Restricting access
We may restrict or remove tier features when an entitlement expires, when a code or payment is reversed or found fraudulent, or for security or abuse reasons, as set out in the [Terms of Service](/docs/terms). Disputes are handled through the Support Server.

## 8. Protect your codes and account
Never share passwords, authentication codes, bot tokens or private dashboard credentials. Only trust information from official Rex links.
